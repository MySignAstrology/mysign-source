Cookies

What we keep in your browser.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Very little. There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies on My Sign, and no analytics company sees your visits. The privacy page covers everything else.

Your choice

Without a yes, we count visits and taps with no random number attached.
