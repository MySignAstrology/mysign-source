Before we begin.

Here’s the honest version, in full.

  • I'll check in each morning, and you can write to me any time.
  • I keep what you tell me so I can refer back to it later. That's the whole point — an app that forgets you is just a horoscope.
  • Your messages are used to make this work for you, and for nothing else. No ads, no selling your data, no third-party trackers.
  • We see which screens you open and which buttons you tap — never what you type — so we can fix what's confusing. We keep that for 90 days. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • You can delete everything, whenever you want, from Settings. It actually deletes.
  • Nobody on our team reads your conversations — except if something you write suggests you could be in danger or that you're under 18, when a person is alerted and may read them, or if you report a reply, which lets us read your recent conversation.
  • I'm not a therapist and not a crisis service. If things get heavy, I'll give you a real line for where you are.
  • You need to be 18 or over.

First, your side.

To read you and someone else, I need you first. We start with a map of the sky at the exact moment and place you were born — astrologers call it your birth chart. The time matters more than people think: it decides your rising sign (how you come across) and which area of your life — love, work, home — each planet speaks to.

Don't know it? — you'll still get your Sun sign and every planet (the Moon too, unless it changed sign that day); you can add the time later for the rest.
