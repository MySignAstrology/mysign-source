What My Sign is

My Sign is a daily companion that reads the sky against your birth chart and remembers what you tell it. It is for reflection, not prediction. It will tell you what a transit (a planet in today's sky touching your chart) means and what you have said before; it will not tell you what is going to happen.

The replies are written by AI. Conversations, readings and the app's memory of your days are generated by Claude, a model made by Anthropic, a third party. To do that, the app sends your messages and related context to Anthropic. The privacy page lists exactly what is sent. The planetary positions are calculated, not generated. The words around them are generated, and AI can be wrong, can misremember, and can sound more certain than it should. Treat what it says as a perspective, not a fact.

What it is not

Not medical, legal, or financial advice. The app is built to refuse those questions, and you should not act on anything it says in those areas.

Not a crisis service. If you say something that suggests you may be in danger, the app stops the astrology and gives you a real crisis line. It also alerts a member of our team, who may read the conversation. That person cannot come and help you, and we do not contact emergency services for you. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Not a substitute for a person. For the big, irreversible decisions — ending a relationship, leaving a job — the app is designed to reflect and to withhold a verdict. That is deliberate.

What you write stays yours. You give us permission to store it, process it, and send it to the providers named on the privacy page, only so that we can run the app for you.
